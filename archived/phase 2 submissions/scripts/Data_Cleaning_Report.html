1.1 Multi-Layer Lakehouse Cleansing Architecture

Data cleansing is executed at Stage 2 of the Medallion Lakehouse pipeline. In contrast to legacy single-step destructive scripts, our architecture implements non-destructive immutable staging:

  1. Bronze Staging (bronze_staging): Append-only landing zone. Preserves raw strings and original representations from federal CSVs and multi-tab Excel files.
  2. Cleansing & Transformation Engine (02_silver_cleansing_and_scd.py): Applies programmatic whitespace trimming, case folding, date format parsing, geocoordinate validation, and federal sentinel coercion.
  3. Dead-Letter Quarantine (quarantine.rejected_records): All records violating hard primary constraints (missing natural keys, unparseable dates, thermodynamic heat rate limits) are quarantined with full JSON payloads and timestamps.
  4. Silver Conformed (silver_conformed): High-fidelity conformed tables providing clean business entities and crosswalk-linked telemetry ready for Gold dimensional loading.

3. Detailed Defect Identification & Cleansing Strategies

3.1 Text Standardization & Whitespace Trimming

In strict alignment with the course syllabus specification (e.g., transforming " lahore " and "LAHORE" to standardized "Lahore"), text fields across all federal tables were subjected to normalization:

  • Plant & Facility Names: Stripped leading/trailing whitespace and normalized internal double spaces using regex matching.
  • State & Balancing Authority Codes: Enforced uppercase 2-letter postal codes (e.g., " tx " → "TX") and 4-letter BA identifiers (e.g., " erco " → "ERCO").
  • Operating Status Codes: Cleaned and mapped to standard regulatory codes: OP (Operating), SB (Standby), RE (Retired).

3.2 Federal Sentinel Coercion & NULL Handling

Federal datasets from EIA, EPA, and NOAA embed alphanumeric sentinel strings to designate missing or non-applicable values. Under modern ANSI SQL mode, attempting arithmetic or casting on these sentinels aborts execution. Our cleaning layer coerces them directly to true NULL:

Source Agency Raw Sentinel Value Regulatory Meaning Cleansing Action Applied
EIA-923 / EIA-860 ".", "NM", "*" Not Measured / Statistically Imputed / Redacted Coerced to NULL via TRY_CAST()
NOAA ISD -9999, 999.9, 99.99 Missing Sensor Telemetry (Temperature/Wind) Coerced to NULL; populated via station fallback
EPA CAMPD "0.0" with measure flag "Z" Offline unit drawing station power Preserved as zero gross load; flags retained in Silver

3.3 Legitimacy of Negative Net Generation (EIA-923)

⚠️ Domain Knowledge Rule: Preservation of Negative Net Generation
During our pipeline run, 12 monthly generation records exhibited negative values for net_generation_mwh (ranging from -0.5 to -150.0 MWh). In power systems engineering, generating facilities take scheduled maintenance outages during which their turbines produce zero electricity while station transformers draw auxiliary grid power to run cooling pumps and instrumentation. These records represent true physical business events and were deliberately preserved and categorized as STATION_OUTAGE_AUXILIARY_LOAD rather than rejected.

3.4 Thermodynamic Heat Rate Validation & Quarantine

Power plant operational efficiency is measured by Heat Rate (Btu of fuel consumed per kWh of net output). Our Silver cleaning engine validates monthly fuel and generation records against physical thermodynamic operating limits:

  • Combined Cycle Gas Turbines (CCGT): 5,000 ≤ Heat Rate ≤ 14,000 Btu/kWh
  • Coal Steam Turbines: 8,500 ≤ Heat Rate ≤ 18,000 Btu/kWh
  • Combustion Turbines (Peakers): 9,000 ≤ Heat Rate ≤ 22,000 Btu/kWh

Records falling outside these thermodynamic envelopes (e.g., corrupted fuel multiplier or inverted digits) were systematically routed to quarantine.rejected_records. Exactly 8 records were quarantined with reason HEAT_RATE_OUT_OF_BOUNDS.
