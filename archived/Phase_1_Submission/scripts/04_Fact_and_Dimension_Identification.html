1. Kimball Dimensional Bus Architecture

A core tenet of Ralph Kimball's dimensional modeling discipline is the Enterprise Bus Architecture. Rather than designing isolated, application-specific data marts, the warehouse coordinates analytical marts through conformed dimensions—dimensions that maintain consistent column names, definitions, and domain values across distinct business processes.

This design integrates three distinct business processes operating at three divergent temporal and physical grains, interconnected via five conformed dimensions:

Business Process Fact Table Fact Archetype dim_date dim_plant dim_generator (Outrigger) dim_balancing_authority dim_weather_station
Smokestack CEMS Continuous Emissions fact_hourly_emissions_weather Transactional X (Hourly) X (Facility) — (via Crosswalk) — (via Plant Attribute) X (≤50 km Spatial Join)
Monthly Plant Generation & Fuel Accounting fact_monthly_plant_operations Periodic Snapshot X (Month Start) X (Facility) — (via Plant Master) — (via Plant Attribute) —
Regional Grid Demand & Fuel Dispatch fact_regional_grid_demand Aggregate Grid X (Hourly) — — X (ISO / RTO) —
Cross-Fact Drill-Across Capability
Although fact_regional_grid_demand operates at the regional balancing authority grain and fact_hourly_emissions_weather operates at the individual smokestack grain, reports can seamlessly drill across both facts. By joining each fact to dim_date and grouping by dim_plant.ba_code = dim_balancing_authority.ba_code, analysts can directly compare total regional electricity demand against aggregate real-time fossil emissions for the identical hour.

4. Surrogate Key Management & Unknown Member Policy

To ensure complete referential stability and insulate the Gold warehouse from operational agency changes:

  1. Surrogate Key Generation: All dimensions utilize integer or BIGINT IDENTITY surrogate keys generated automatically upon insert. Natural business keys (e.g., orispl_code, ba_code, station_id) are retained alongside surrogate keys with UNIQUE constraints.
  2. Default Unknown Member (Row -1): Every dimension table contains a pre-populated row with surrogate key -1, textual descriptions set to 'Unknown / Unspecified', and coordinates set to NULL. When fact ETL encounters an unmapped natural key, it maps the foreign key to -1 rather than dropping the record or throwing referential exceptions.
